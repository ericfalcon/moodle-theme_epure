A modern, clean and accessible theme for **Moodle 4.5 LTS to 5.3** and **IOMAD 4.5 to 5.2**, child of Boost. Few settings: choose an identity, Épure computes the rest. A **getting started** guides the first settings. **Free and open source** (GNU GPL v3): no paid version.

### One colour, an accessible palette

Choose a brand colour (or click one in your logo): Épure derives the whole palette and keeps text at 4.5:1 at least, in light and dark mode. White, coloured or gradient header.

### Accessibility built in

- Display preferences for every user, visitors included (« Aa »): text up to 200 %, dyslexia-friendly fonts, spacing, short lines, high contrast, reduced motion, reading ruler or mask, dark, yellow on black or cream, reading aloud with the local voices of the device. Profiles in one click: Dyslexia, Low vision, Concentration, No flashing.
- Pages that reflow down to 320 px at 200 %, focus always visible, 24 px targets, keyboard shortcuts.
- An accessibility statement filled in step by step, and a public page « What Épure covers »: the whole grid of the referential (106 RGAA criteria, EN 301 549 or WCAG 2.2), who answers each criterion and its state in a reference pre-audit, to download.
- A content checker as teachers write, and a guide « Accessibility of the course »: every activity and the 47 most installed activity plugins, Office and PDF documents, SCORM and IMS packages (Storyline, Rise, HTML); each mistake with its severity, a score out of 100 %, fixes made from the guide; two capabilities for who uses it and who confirms.
- The accessibility of all the courses on a management dashboard, week by week, with alerts if the computation fails.
- Defects of Moodle repaired where a theme can (calendar, forms, tables, editor, status messages…), several found by an RGAA audit and the Moodle LMS VPAT.
- Automated pre-audit (axe-core, WCAG 2.2 AA) of 76 pages in every display, on every change; a reference RGAA pre-audit on Moodle 4.5 and 5.3: 80 criteria conform, none non-conform, 9 for an auditor to verify.

### For learners

- A dashboard: where to resume, what is due this week, what is completed; a page « To do » across all courses.
- Progress per section, previous and next activity, a reading mode, new activities marked « New ».
- A preview of files without leaving the course, with the text of Office and PDF documents, read aloud.
- « My summary »: a tagged PDF (PDF/UA) of courses, grades and certificates, with a verification code the learner can revoke.
- A bottom navigation bar on phones and an installable web app.

### For teachers and administrators

- Your own words in all Moodle (« trainee », « trainer », « learning path »…), undone in one click.
- « My courses » as a table: learners to follow up, completion, work to grade, accessibility score.
- A management dashboard (also per IOMAD company): activity, courses most followed, completion, accessibility.
- « Épure and personal data » for the data protection officer: what the theme keeps, GDPR responsibilities, an extract for the record. No tracker, no outside service.
- Quick search (Ctrl+K), settings in tabs, a clearer administration home, long forms easier to save.
- A footer written in the editor, branded e-mails and a certificate template.

### With IOMAD

Each company has its own colours, logo, font, vocabulary, accessibility statement and management dashboard.

### Compatibility

Moodle 4.5 to 5.3, IOMAD 4.5 to 5.2, PHP 8.1 to 8.4, PostgreSQL and MariaDB, tested in continuous integration. Privacy API; fonts and libraries bundled.

An automated pre-audit does not replace a manual audit. Épure is independent, not affiliated with Moodle HQ or IOMAD. Documentation: <https://github.com/ericfalcon/moodle-theme_epure>
